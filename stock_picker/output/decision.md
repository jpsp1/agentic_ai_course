After reviewing the available research and ensuring the analysis is aligned with the current date of 2026-10-21, the best company for investment is **Microsoft**.

## Why Microsoft was chosen

Microsoft stands out as the strongest overall investment candidate because it combines:
- **Dominant enterprise software leadership**
- **A major cloud platform in Azure**
- **Strong AI distribution through Microsoft 365 and enterprise copilots**
- **Improving AI infrastructure economics through Maia 300**

The key reason Microsoft ranks first is that it is not just exposed to AI demand — it is positioned to **monetize AI across multiple layers of the stack**:
1. **Infrastructure layer:** Azure benefits from continued cloud and AI compute demand.
2. **Software layer:** Microsoft 365 and other enterprise products provide broad distribution for AI features.
3. **Efficiency layer:** Maia 300 may reduce AI compute costs and improve margins over time.

This combination gives Microsoft both **growth and resilience**. Its business is diversified, its customer base is sticky, and its AI strategy is more directly tied to revenue and operating leverage than the others in the list. While the stock may already reflect some AI optimism, its strategic position and long-term compounding potential still make it the best overall choice.

## Why the other companies were not selected

### Apple
Apple is a high-quality company with a powerful ecosystem, premium brand, and strong cash generation. It is especially compelling as a lower-risk long-term compounder. However, it was not selected over Microsoft because:
- Its near-term upside depends heavily on product-cycle execution, especially the October launch event.
- A lot of Apple’s value already comes from its mature hardware ecosystem.
- Its AI monetization path is less clear and less directly monetizable than Microsoft’s enterprise and cloud AI stack.
- The investment case is solid, but more dependent on product refreshes and valuation sensitivity.

In short, Apple is excellent, but Microsoft offers a broader and more immediate AI-driven growth runway.

### ABB
ABB is well positioned to benefit from AI-driven data-center buildout, electrification, and power infrastructure demand. It is an attractive industrial AI-enabler. However, it was not selected because:
- Its upside is more indirect and tied to infrastructure spending cycles.
- There are meaningful concerns about financing the AI buildout and capital intensity.
- The stock may face more volatility if sentiment around data-center expansion weakens.
- It is more cyclical and less scalable than Microsoft’s software/cloud model.

ABB remains a strong company, but its opportunity is narrower and more exposed to execution and financing risks than Microsoft.

## Final decision
**Selected company: Microsoft**

**Reason:** Microsoft offers the strongest combination of durable competitive advantage, diversified growth drivers, AI monetization potential, and improving long-term economics through Maia 300. It is the best balance of quality, growth, and strategic positioning among the three companies reviewed.
